Educational Guide: Single-Cell Transcriptomics & UMAP Dimensionality Reduction

1. Motivation & Biological Background

Traditional bulk RNA sequencing measures average gene expression across millions of pooled cells, hiding critical cell-to-cell variations. Single-Cell RNA Sequencing (scRNA-seq) encapsulates individual cells into microfluidic droplets (such as 10x Genomics Chromium), enabling high-throughput profiling of thousands of individual cell transcriptomes simultaneously.

Cellular Heterogeneity

Discovers rare cell subtypes, immune cell sub-states, and stem cell differentiation lineages.

Tumor Microenvironment

Maps malignant cell clones alongside infiltrating T-cells, B-cells, and stromal macrophages in biopsies.

Developmental Trajectories

Reconstructs pseudotime developmental continuums during organogenesis and embryo formation.

2. Single-Cell Barcoding & UMI PCR Deduplication

Microfluidic gel beads contain oligos with two distinct barcode sequences:

  • Cell Barcode (CB, ~16 bp): Identifies the cell of origin.
  • Unique Molecular Identifier (UMI, ~10-12 bp): Tagged to individual mRNA transcript molecules prior to PCR amplification to eliminate PCR duplication bias.

3. Quality Control Filtering & Library Normalization

Cells with low gene counts ($<200$) or high mitochondrial gene expression ($MT\% > 10\%$, indicative of dying/lysed cells) are removed. Expression values are then normalized per cell to a target library size of $10,000$ counts and log-transformed:

$$X_{i, j} = \log \left( 1 + \frac{C_{i, j}}{\sum_k C_{i, k}} \times 10,000 \right)$$

4. Dimensionality Reduction & Non-Linear UMAP Projection

From ~20,000 total genes, the top 2,000 Highly Variable Genes (HVGs) are selected. Linear Principal Component Analysis (PCA) reduces dimensions to 30 PCs, followed by non-linear Uniform Manifold Approximation and Projection (UMAP) graph layout to visualize cell clusters in 2D space.

6. Scanpy / Python 3 Reference Implementation

Production-ready, type-annotated Python 3 pipeline using Scanpy and AnnData:

import scanpy as sc
import anndata as ad

def process_scrna_pipeline(matrix_path: str) -> ad.AnnData:
    """Standard Scanpy scRNA-seq processing pipeline."""
    adata = sc.read_10x_h5(matrix_path)
    
    # Step 1: QC filtering
    sc.pp.filter_cells(adata, min_genes=200)
    sc.pp.filter_genes(adata, min_cells=3)
    
    # Step 2: Normalization & log transformation
    sc.pp.normalize_total(adata, target_sum=1e4)
    sc.pp.log1p(adata)
    
    # Step 3: Feature selection (HVGs)
    sc.pp.highly_variable_genes(adata, n_top_genes=2000)
    
    # Step 4: PCA & Neighbor graph embedding
    sc.tl.pca(adata, svd_solver='arpack')
    sc.pp.neighbors(adata, n_neighbors=15, n_pcs=30)
    
    # Step 5: Non-linear UMAP & Leiden clustering
    sc.tl.umap(adata)
    sc.tl.leiden(adata, resolution=0.5)
    
    return adata
